What this is

The update, without the backsliding.

A big Windows feature update (like 24H2→25H2) often quietly switches the bloat back on—Copilot, Recall, telemetry, the lot.  This re-applies the RR Turbo cleanup right after the update, so your machine comes out the far side exactly as private and lean as it went in.

What it does
  • Moves Windows 11 from 24H2 to 25H2
  • Re-applies the RR Turbo debloat afterward
  • Keeps your files, apps, and settings
  • Walks you through it, step by step
What it never does
  • Turn Copilot or Recall back on
  • Re-enable telemetry you had removed
  • Touch or upload your personal files
  • Lock you in — you can see every step
How to run it

Three steps, then a restart.

1
Extract the zip

Right-click the downloaded RR-Update-25H2.zip and choose Extract All.

2
Double-click RR-Update

It checks your version and walks you through the update in plain language.  Plug in a laptop; leave it alone while it works.

3
Restart when it asks

Windows finishes on reboot and the cleanup re-applies itself.  You’re current—and still clean.

One thing you might see

A blue “Windows protected your PC” box

The first time you run it, Windows may show a blue box that says “Windows protected your PC.”  That’s SmartScreen being cautious with any small, unsigned tool—not a warning that something is wrong.  Click More info, then Run anyway.  Prefer to read it first?  It’s plain text you can open in Notepad before you run.
