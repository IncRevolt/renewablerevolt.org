What this is

A private AI that answers to you.

The same assistant that ships in our machines, free for the computer you already own.  It runs on your graphics card, in your house—so it works with the internet unplugged, and nothing you ask it is ever sent anywhere.

What it does
  • Chat — a real assistant for writing, questions, and everyday work
  • Make images from a description, right on your machine
  • On an 8GB card, read a screenshot or photo and tell you what’s in it
  • Run entirely offline, on your own graphics card
What it never does
  • Ask for an account or a sign-in
  • Charge a subscription — it’s free and yours
  • Send your words, documents, or images to the cloud
  • Need the internet once it’s installed
How to run it

Three steps. Then it’s just yours.

1
Extract the zip

Right-click the downloaded RR-AI.zip and choose Extract All.  You’ll get one folder.

2
Double-click Start-RR-AI

Open the folder and double-click it.  The first run sets itself up; after that it starts in seconds.

3
Your browser opens — start chatting

Your private AI opens in your own browser, running on your machine.  No login, no setup.  Close the window when you’re done.

One thing you might see

A blue “Windows protected your PC” box

The first time you run it, Windows may show a blue box that says “Windows protected your PC.”  That’s SmartScreen being cautious with any small, unsigned download—not a sign anything is wrong.  Click More info, then Run anyway.  Everything it installs stays in its own folder and can be deleted anytime.

Will it run on my PC?

If it can game, it can probably think.

RR-AI runs best on a machine with an 8GB graphics card (the Marauder’s class) and RR Turbo Windows 11.  A 6GB card runs the lighter assistant; the image-reading model wants the 8GB.  Not sure what you’ve got?  The free checker reads your specs and tells you in a minute.
