Our Mission

End of support isn’t end of life.

When an OEM walks away from a machine, we don’t.  We recover, revive, and redeploy hardware the industry calls obsolete—and prove it has years of real performance left.  Gaming PCs keep the lights on; donations put private, capable machines in the hands of veterans, students, and families who need them.
