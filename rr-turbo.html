What It Is

The hardware and the AI,engineered together.

A working local-AI stack can’t be bolted onto a random PC.  It needs the right class of GPU and enough memory, drivers that behave, a Windows build tuned to hand the model every usable megabyte of video memory, and an engine stack matched exactly to that hardware.  Miss any one and you’re back to the crashes and crawls that make people quit.

RR Turbo is that foundation.  The hardware and the AI are specified together, imaged together, and tested together on real machines across every memory tier before a unit ships.  The same capable gaming and workstation cards the industry discards are precisely enough to run a modern, compressed model—if, and only if, the whole system is engineered around that goal.  That engineered system is the product.  No spec sheet copies it.

It runs offline, with a local account.  No Microsoft sign-in required, no telemetry, and the AI runs entirely on the machine—unplug the network and it keeps working.  LibreWolf (privacy-hardened) is the browser; Office 2019 Pro+ is included on the machines we build.  And it stays current: a free, guided update carries RR Turbo through each new Windows release without undoing the cleanup—see the updater →

Straight Talk

The honest boundaries.

Credibility sells this; hype doesn’t—so here’s the fine print, plainly.

The software is free and as-is.  RR Turbo v7 is MIT-licensed and provided as-is—standard for free, open software.  Known issues are posted publicly, and OS-level problems are handled by Windows / Microsoft.  The image and coding models are open-source community models under their own licenses.

The local models are strong, not frontier.  They’re roughly the capability most people need day to day—private, offline, and owned.  For the hardest frontier reasoning, the big cloud models still lead; RR Turbo trades a little peak capability for total privacy, offline use, and no subscription.

The machines are refurbished.  That’s the point—sustainability and affordability.  Exact parts vary by unit, each matched to its tier and validated before shipping, and machines we build carry a limited hardware warranty against defects.
